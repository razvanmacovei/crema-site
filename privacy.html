Crema

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Crema is an app for Bluetooth coffee scales, made by an independent developer. Crema has no accounts, shows no ads, collects no usage statistics and does not track you. This page explains the little that leaves your phone and why.

What stays on your phone

Your recipes, your shot history and notes, your settings, and the Bluetooth identifier of the last scale you connected are stored only on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Bluetooth

Crema uses Bluetooth only to find supported coffee scales nearby and to read their weight. It does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

What is sent, and why

WhatWhat it containsWhy
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)What the app was doing when it crashed or when a scale failed to connect, the scale model, your phone model and system version, the app version, and a random identifier for this installation. Bluetooth addresses of devices are removed before sending.To find and fix bugs.
Feedback (Cloud Firestore)Only when you send feedback: the kind, what you wrote, and the scale model if you request one, plus the app version and whether your phone is an iPhone or Android. If Attach app details is on, also your system version, the connected scale model, whether Bluetooth is on, and how many shots you have saved.To read and answer your feedback. Nobody else can read it.

Crema does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. If you include personal details in a feedback note yourself, they are stored with that note.

Turning it off

Crash reports can be turned off at any time: open the recipe sheet (Edit under the recipe on the main screen) and switch off Help improve Crema. Feedback is only sent when you choose to send it.

Who processes the data

Crash reports and feedback are handled by Google through Firebase, as a service provider for Crema, and may be stored on servers in the United States. Google's terms are at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

How long it is kept

  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Feedback: until it has been dealt with, and no longer than 2 years. You can ask for it to be deleted sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to its use. Crash reports are processed on the basis of a legitimate interest in keeping the app working, and you can switch them off as described above. Feedback is processed because you chose to send it. Because Crema has no accounts, the data is not tied to your name; write to the address below and describe what you sent, and it will be found and handled where possible. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Crema is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app starts collecting something new, this page will say so before that version is released.
