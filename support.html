Support

Questions, problems, or a scale you would like Crema to support.

Get in touch

Write to razvan.macovei95@gmail.com. You can also use Send feedback or request a scale at the bottom of the app's main screen.

When you report a problem, it helps to say which phone you use, which scale, and what you did just before it went wrong.

Phones

Crema needs iOS 17 or newer on iPhone, and Android 12 or newer on Android.

The scale does not appear

  • Wake the scale: most scales turn Bluetooth off when they are asleep.
  • A scale accepts only one connection at a time. If it is connected to its own app or to another phone, disconnect it there first, or close that app.
  • Check that Bluetooth is on and that Crema is allowed to use it, in your phone's settings.
  • Only scales whose protocol is known are listed. If yours is missing, request it from the app.

Your data

Recipes and shot history are kept only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy for what is sent and how to ask for it to be deleted.
